1氯气泄露
Chlorine gas leakage
氢气泄露
Hydrogen gas leakage		目前流路设计核心氯气发生部分是参考HASA原型机设计，氯气混合装置与盐水箱联通，当氯气排放受阻（例如增压水泵停止工作，外部循环水泵停止工作等一系列管路堵塞情况发生），氯气将会从电极底部和混合装置开口处溢出并流入盐水箱中与里面的液体发生反应，多余的氯气和氢气会在机器内部和外部扩散；DVT样机在美国现场部署时因为反应槽有微弱电流通过，发生氯气和氢气泄露的问题。
Currently, the core chlorine generation part of the flow path design is based on the HASA prototype. The chlorine mixing device is connected to the brine tank. When chlorine discharge is obstructed (for example, when the booster pump stops working, the external circulation pump stops working, or a series of other pipeline blockage situations occur), chlorine will overflow from the bottom of the electrode and the opening of the mixing device and flow into the brine tank, reacting with the liquid inside. Excess chlorine and hydrogen will diffuse both inside and outside the machine. When the DVT prototype was deployed on site in the United States, because a weak current passed through the reaction tank, a chlorine and hydrogen leakage problem occurred.		需要更多的测试，以便了解产品的状况；
More testing is needed in order to understand the condition of the product.
2盐水酸碱平衡
Salt water acid-base balance		在DVT*10台机器的流路设计中，参考HASA原型机器设计了整个水路，包含：反应槽的阳极气液分离和阴极气液分离为纯物理分离、整个水箱的水容量、水箱上接头高低的位置、水位的高低等，酸碱都汇入盐水箱保持平衡有很大操控难度。无法确定在整个系统在运行过程中会有多少碱液从阴极流回盐水桶以及有多少酸液从阳极流回盐水桶。在DVT的10台机器测试中，发现运行10小时以后盐水都偏向于碱性(PH>10)，具体盐水的酸碱程度最终是否会保持稳定则需要测试更多的数据，且盐水变酸和盐水变碱后对电极和离子交换膜的影响也需要长期验证；另外尽管我们考虑后期通过使用电动球阀D来排放碱液，但盐水箱上面碱液排放口的高度与正常液位只相差2cm,这意味着碱液排放量有限，是否可以达到调节盐水PH的效果需要做更多的验证和测试；
In the flow path design of the 10 DVT machines, the entire water circuit was designed with reference to the HASA prototype machine, including: the anode gas-liquid separation and cathode gas-liquid separation of the reaction tank as purely physical separation, the water capacity of the entire water tank, the heights of the upper fittings on the water tank, the high and low water levels, etc. Both acid and alkali flow into the brine tank to maintain balance, which is very difficult to control. It is impossible to determine how much lye will flow back from the cathode into the brine tank and how much acid will flow back from the anode into the brine tank during operation of the entire system. In the testing of the 10 DVT machines, it was found that after 10 hours of operation, the brine tended to become alkaline (pH > 10). Whether the acidity and alkalinity of the brine will ultimately remain stable requires more data to be tested, and the effects of the brine becoming acidic or alkaline on the electrodes and ion exchange membrane also require long-term verification. In addition, although we considered later using electric ball valve D to discharge the lye, the height of the lye discharge port above the brine tank differs from the normal liquid level by only 2 cm, which means the lye discharge volume is limited. Whether this can achieve the effect of adjusting the pH of the brine requires more verification and testing.		需要更多的测试，以便了解产品的状况；
More testing is needed in order to understand the condition of the product.
3反应槽的可靠性
Reliability of the reaction tank		DVT的10台样机使用的反应槽是供应商按照HASA原来的反应槽复制生产出来的，内部的膜使用的是东岳的膜。出货前的功能测试中（两个电极分别施加15A电流持续工作12小时），结果发现盐水箱内的水变成浅红色，发现氯气产量变低（1000L水箱，15A持续10分钟余氯产量3ppm左右），拆解反应槽发现内部的膜出现腐蚀并制氯量降低；在此基础上重新更换干净的自来水和相同的盐测试氯气产量，恢复正常；反应槽在实际用户环境使用中的可靠性需要跟多的测试来验证；
The reaction tanks used in the 10 DVT prototypes were replicated and produced by the supplier based on HASA's original reaction tank, and the membranes inside used Dongyue membranes. During the pre-shipment functional test (two electrodes each applied with 15A current for 12 hours of continuous operation), it was found that the water in the brine tank turned light red, and the chlorine production was found to be lower (1000L water tank, 15A continuous for 10 minutes, residual chlorine production around 3ppm). Disassembly of the reaction tank revealed that the internal membrane had corroded and the chlorine production rate had decreased; on this basis, after replacing with clean tap water and the same salt for retesting the chlorine production, it returned to normal; the reliability of the reaction tank in actual user environments needs more testing to verify.		短期：所有测试样机在测试中使用纯净的水和纯净的盐，排除测试干扰；
长期：后续老化测试中搜集更多盐水温度变化，盐水酸碱度变化，以及电解电极电压变化数据，当电极效率下降后分析原因；
Short term: All test prototypes use purified water and purified salt during testing to eliminate test interference.
Long term: In subsequent aging tests, collect more data on brine temperature changes, brine pH changes, and electrolysis electrode voltage changes. When electrode efficiency declines, analyze the cause.
4传感器协同配合问题
Sensor collaboration and coordination issues		目前产品增加较多智能传感器(PH/ORP/EC/流量计/压力传感器等)以及电动球阀，软件的判断逻辑和如何使用这些数据有很大工作量，初步看来有两大挑战：1、传感器需要保持精度稳定，这需要在售后得到良好的维护和良好的可靠性（校准和维护以及传感器本身品质）；2、软件在使用这些数据进行判断之前需要采集更多数据去设定具体阈值，且需要反复验证（在V1.10.132上的流量计判断可以证明）；做到以上两点才能有效避免软件误判并有效利用这些传感器数据；
Currently, the product has added quite a few smart sensors (pH/ORP/EC/flow meters/pressure sensors, etc.) as well as electric ball valves. The software's judgment logic and how to use this data involve a significant workload. From an initial perspective, there are two major challenges: 1. The sensors need to maintain stable accuracy, which requires good after-sales maintenance and good reliability (calibration and maintenance, as well as the quality of the sensors themselves); 2. Before the software uses this data for judgment, it needs to collect more data to set specific thresholds, and repeated verification is required (the flow meter judgment on V1.10.132 can prove this); only by achieving the above two points can software misjudgments be effectively avoided and the sensor data be effectively utilized.		1，定义清晰的传感器数据的使用规则；
2，收集现有传感器大量数据；
3，将收集到的数据按照使用规则做反复的逻辑修改和验证；
1,Define clear rules for the use of sensor data;
2,Collect large amounts of data from existing sensors;
3,Repeatedly revise and verify the logic of the collected data in accordance with the usage rules.
5减重和缩小体积的局限性
Limitations of weight reduction and volume reduction		目前产品中的液位高度，水箱孔位高度，盐水容量和电解电极结构设计均参考HASA原型机设计，这在一定程度上限制了在结构设计中的水箱尺寸，从而影响整机的尺寸和重量控制；需要构思产品的整体结构和布局优化，例如在之前的山东次氯酸钠发生器考察中，我们看到很多类似产品采用盐水箱和流路及控制部分上下分离的模式，这样设计可以更好做防腐处理和整机高度控制，但在我们现有产品上是否可以实现需要进一步构思；
Currently, the liquid level height in the product, the height of the water tank holes, the brine capacity, and the structural design of the electrolysis electrodes all reference the HASA prototype design. To a certain extent, this constrains the water tank dimensions in the structural design, thereby affecting the overall size and weight control of the machine. It is necessary to conceive an optimization of the product's overall structure and layout. For example, during a previous inspection visit regarding a sodium hypochlorite generator in Shandong, we saw that many similar products adopt a vertically separated configuration in which the brine tank, the flow path, and the control section are arranged one above the other. This design allows for better corrosion protection and better control of the overall machine height. However, whether this can be implemented in our existing product requires further consideration.		产品整体设计重新构思：框架改为碳纤维或去除框架，框架从20×20×2mm改为20×20×1.2mm；气体混合模块移入水箱；电源从水平改垂直安装；托盘做薄；控制箱做小或拆分为2个；高度减少10cm；更换更小轮子；改为2万向轮+2定向轮， 改成上下结构方式，同时需要考虑扁平化和模块化运输需求；
Redesign of the overall product structure: change the frame to carbon fiber or remove the frame entirely; change the frame from 20×20×2 mm to 20×20×1.2 mm; move the gas mixing module into the water tank; change the power supply from horizontal to vertical mounting; make the tray thinner; make the control box smaller or split it into two; reduce the height by 10 cm; replace the wheels with smaller ones; change to 2 swivel casters + 2 fixed casters; adopt a top-and-bottom structural arrangement, while also taking into account the requirements for flat-packing and modular transportation.
6整机部件防腐蚀
All Components Corrosion Risk		在过去的测试中，我们使用的203不锈钢，304不锈钢和316不锈钢都出现不同程度腐蚀，这使得我们后续选择金属部件中需要更加慎重。如果有些金属部件不得不使用，成本和方案选型将是一个巨大的挑战；另外我们的腐蚀环境并不固定，因此很难给供应商提供具体的环境腐蚀程度数据作为参考这些都；
In previous tests, the 203 stainless steel, 304 stainless steel, and 316 stainless steel we used all showed varying degrees of corrosion, which requires us to be more cautious when selecting metal components in the future. If some metal components have to be used, the cost and selection difficulty are very high. In addition, my corrosion environment is not fixed, so it is difficult to provide suppliers with specific environmental corrosion degree data as a reference. These are all things we need to consider;		产品整体设计重新构思，
材料重新选择,原则：确立“非受力件优先用塑料”的原则，仅在必要时使用金属，且需做好防腐处理（如阳极氧化+喷漆）
The overall product design should be reconceived, and materials should be reselected. Principle: Establish the principle of "prioritizing plastics for non-load-bearing parts," using metal only when necessary, and ensuring proper anti-corrosion treatment (such as anodizing + painting).
7认证与合规风险
Certification Risk		PRD要求认证：NSF/ANSI 50、UL/ETL、FCC、RoHS、REACH、Prop 65等建议确认现阶段的认证是否要做，全部做还是只做其中的部分；
Suggest to confirm whether the certifications at the current stage need to be pursued, and whether to pursue all of them or only some of them.
